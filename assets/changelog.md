## 1.0.0 — The first Zolt

- Schematic capture and board layout, with KiCad in and out
- Board outlines drawn with the Edge cut tool, with soft snaps to corners,
  middles and centres
- Arcs drawn by their centre, start and end
- Silkscreen text in ten fonts, or your own .ttf
- Pictures on the silkscreen, from a picture library
- Component IDs for assembly, in the BOM as LCSC Part #
- Slotted holes drilled as slots
- Fab-ready Gerbers, drill files, BOM and pick-and-place
